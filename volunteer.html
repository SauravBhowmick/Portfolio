Volunteer

Rotaractor at Rotaract Club Freiburg

Currently engaging in various community service activities with Rotaract Club Freiburg, including a book donation drive for literacy initiatives, catering for club events, and an environmental restoration project in the Black Forest where I help plant trees. These experiences allow me to contribute to both social and environmental causes while connecting with like-minded individuals dedicated to community service and sustainable development.

Rotaractor at Rotaract Club of Pune Camp Pioneers

During my active membership with the Rotaract Club of Pune Camp Pioneers, I participated in several impactful humanitarian initiatives. I contributed to educational access by donating essential stationery items to two notable NGOs: Door Step School and Care Foundation (Emmanuel Public School). These donations helped provide necessary learning materials to underprivileged students, supporting their educational journey. Additionally, when the Mahad region was devastated by flooding, I contributed financially to relief efforts coordinated by our club. This monetary donation helped provide emergency supplies, temporary shelter, and other critical resources to affected families during their time of need. These experiences reinforced my commitment to community service and demonstrated the importance of collective action in addressing both ongoing societal needs and responding to unexpected crises.

Content Writer at Coders Evoke

During my volunteer role at Coders Evoke, I took on significant responsibilities in their digital marketing efforts by creating engaging advertisement posts. I developed compelling visual and written content strategically designed to promote the organization's events, workshops, and technical initiatives to the broader coding community. My work involved crafting eye-catching graphics and persuasive copy that effectively communicated the value proposition of Coders Evoke's offerings while maintaining consistent brand messaging. Through careful attention to design principles and marketing psychology, I helped increase engagement metrics across their social media platforms. This experience enhanced my creative skills while allowing me to contribute meaningfully to an organization dedicated to fostering coding education and community building among technology enthusiasts.
