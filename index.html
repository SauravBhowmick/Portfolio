About Me

Hello! I'm Saurav Bhowmick, a passionate Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with 5+ years of experience in software development, automation, and data engineering across global tech environments. I recently completed my MSc in Renewable Energy and Data Engineering at Hochschule Offenburg, where I specialized in integrating data-driven solutions with sustainable energy technologies.

With a 95% project success rate, my experience spans renewable energy research, automotive industry applications, and cloud solutions. I've worked at Volkswagen Group Technology Solutions India, served as a Research Assistant at the Institute for Sustainable Energy Systems, and contributed to innovative projects at ySpace and Grydion. My expertise includes machine learning, data visualization, IoT integration, power electronics, and industrial automation using Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and advanced analytics tools.

Beyond my professional work, I'm actively involved in community service through Rotaract Club, enjoy playing chess and football, read about technology and innovation, and maintain an active lifestyle through jogging. My goal is to contribute to the advancement of renewable energy solutions and data-driven innovation through AI, machine learning, and automation.
