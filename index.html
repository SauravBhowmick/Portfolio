Institute for Sustainable Energy Systems (INES)

Master Thesis Student & Research Assistant

Master Thesis Student (September 2024 - February 2025): I served as a Master's Thesis Student on the Integrale Forschung Energie für Elektromobilität: Vom Modell zur Anwendung (iFEMA) project. My research focused on "Analysis and Prediction of Power Quality Anomalies in Power Electronic Dominated Industrial Systems." I employed cross-correlation analysis, curve fitting techniques (sine wave applications), and GridVis software for effective anomaly detection. I successfully identified the most causally significant features contributing to power quality anomalies, which formed the foundation for developing machine learning models capable of forecasting grid quality issues. To address the inherent challenge of imbalanced data in anomaly detection, I implemented specialized imbalanced learning techniques, complementing these with neural network architectures designed for voltage predictions. The technical stack for this work encompassed Python, Node-RED, InfluxDB, TensorFlow, Scikit-Learn, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn.

Research Assistant (January - August 2024): I developed Python scripts that established reliable communication pathways between Beckhoff PLCs and MQTT servers, creating a robust foundation for industrial IoT applications. I successfully implemented code to publish messages from PLCs to MQTT servers and capture messages from MQTT servers to send to PLCs. I automated critical data logging processes from both PLC systems and MQTT topics into MySQL databases, significantly enhancing data collection efficiency by 40%. My contributions to IoT-based data engineering solutions enabled real-time energy monitoring capabilities. I also successfully transferred variables from MQTT to PLC and back via Node-RED, automated JSON creation for Node-RED, and connected PLCs with Modbus and OPC-UA protocols through Node-RED, leveraging technologies including Python, MQTT, MySQL, Beckhoff TwinCAT (Structured Text), and Node-RED to create an integrated monitoring ecosystem.
